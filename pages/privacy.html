Privacy & Security Policy

Your digital safety is our mission.

1. Data Collection

Wlan0tto Cyber Hub is designed with privacy as a core principle. We operate as a static site and do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

2. Local Storage

For administrative features and theme preferences, we use your browser's Local Storage. This data remains on your device and is never shared with us or third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party CDNs (Content Delivery Networks) for performance:

  • Tailwind CSS: For styling.
  • Google Fonts: For typography.

4. Communications

When you contact us via WhatsApp, your interaction is governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy. We treat all client communications as strictly confidential.
